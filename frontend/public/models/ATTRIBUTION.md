Changes for this prototype: embedded 2048px PNG texture resized to 1024px JPEG at quality 80; GLB repacked. Suit controls and stripes recolored in Qhipa Pacha purple and orange. Geometry unchanged. Local file: astronaut.glb.

This is a visual reference, not an anatomically validated medical model. Neither Google nor NASA endorses ASTRA.

## Procedural illustrations

Earth, Moon and Mars surfaces, solar and planetary context illustrations, the orbital station, spacecraft and habitat modules are original procedural illustrations generated by this prototype. They use local Three.js geometry and canvas textures without external assets. They do not represent accurate geography, orbital scale, real-time positions or engineering interiors. All named habitats and crew are fictional demo scenarios.

Los accesorios de personalización de `avatar/` se construyeron con geometría de Three.js para Qhipa Pacha. Las texturas WebP del traje son variantes del mismo modelo de Poly atribuido arriba.
