## Procedural illustrations

Earth, Moon and Mars surfaces, solar and planetary context illustrations, the orbital station, spacecraft and habitat modules are original procedural illustrations generated by this prototype. They use local Three.js geometry and canvas textures without external assets. They do not represent accurate geography, orbital scale, real-time positions or engineering interiors. All named habitats and crew are fictional demo scenarios.

## Human hologram

`human-hologram.glb` derives from the MakeHuman Community base mesh, explicitly released as **CC0 1.0 Universal**. Original asset holders listed in the OBJ: Data Collection AB, Joel Palmius and Jonas Hauquier (2020).

- Source: https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/blob/master/makehuman/data/3dobjs/base.obj
- License evidence: https://github.com/makehumancommunity/makehuman/blob/master/LICENSE.md (section C).
- Full license included in `human-hologram.LICENSE.md`.
- Source OBJ SHA256: `8e761e6624b8f54536409135d1636da63b32486a90d4897f84e121d144f6fb4c`.
- Changes: keep only the `body` group, remove helper geometry, triangulate, compute smooth normals, center vertically and scale to 2.7 scene units. Convert to a self-contained GLB without textures, rig or external files. 13,380 vertices, 26,756 triangles, about 471 KiB.
- Reproduce with `python3 scripts/prepare-human-hologram.py /path/to/base.obj` from the repository root.

ASTRA supplies its own holographic shader and interactive overlays. This generic figure is a visual reference, not a scan of a crew member or an anatomically validated medical model. The reference image supplied in chat is not bundled as an asset.

### Appearance variants

`human-hologram-masculine.glb` and `human-hologram-feminine.glb` apply the matching MakeHuman `macrodetails/*-male-young.target` / `*-female-young.target` displacements before normalization. The three macrodetail bases are averaged with equal weights to create generic visual figures; no ancestry, identity or medical attributes are assigned to crew members. These targets are CC0 assets under the same included license. Exact source URLs, weights and SHA256 hashes are recorded in `human-hologram-variants.sources.json`. Both variants preserve the topology (13,380 vertices / 26,756 triangles) and the holographic shader. Each local GLB is about 471 KiB and only the selected variant is requested.
